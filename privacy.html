Privacy Policy

How SentinelAI collects, uses and protects data within hotel premises.

Effective Date: 1 January 2025
Last Updated: April 2025
Applies To: All Authorized SentinelAI Deployments

1. Information We Collect

SentinelAI collects and processes the following categories of data during system operation:

  • Live and recorded surveillance footage from hotel premises cameras.
  • Incident logs including type, location, severity, timestamp and resolution status.
  • Staff assignment and response records linked to specific incidents.
  • System performance metrics such as response times and resolution rates.
  • Alert snapshot images captured at the moment of incident detection.

2. How We Use Your Data

All collected data is used exclusively for the following purposes within the licensed hotel premises:

  • Real-time detection and classification of security and safety incidents.
  • Automated dispatch and coordination of the appropriate response personnel.
  • Live monitoring and status tracking across the crisis response lifecycle.
  • Post-incident report generation for management review and future planning.

3. Data Storage & Retention

All data is stored exclusively on the hotel's local server infrastructure. No data is transmitted to external servers, cloud platforms or third parties during normal operation.

  • Alert snapshot images are retained for a default period of 30 days.
  • Incident logs and response records are retained for 12 months.
  • Archived reports are retained as long as required by hotel policy or applicable regulations.

4. Security Measures

Access to the SentinelAI Command Center is restricted to authorized personnel through credential-based authentication. All system data remains within the hotel's secure local network. Administrators are responsible for maintaining the security of access credentials and should report any unauthorized access immediately.

5. Camera Surveillance Notice

Guests, staff and visitors of the premises are subject to video monitoring in designated areas in accordance with applicable local laws and posted notices. Surveillance is conducted strictly for safety and security purposes and is not used for any form of profiling unrelated to incident detection.

6. Third-Party Access

SentinelAI does not share, sell or transmit any collected data to third parties. Emergency services may receive incident information only when manually authorized by designated hotel management personnel during an active emergency situation.

7. Your Rights

Authorized users and affected parties may request access to incident data related to them, subject to hotel policy and applicable law. Requests should be directed to the hotel's designated system administrator.

8. Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated to reflect changes in system capabilities, hotel policy or regulatory requirements. Continued use of SentinelAI following any updates constitutes acceptance of the revised policy. Material changes will be communicated to system administrators.

9. Contact

For privacy-related inquiries, please contact your hotel's designated SentinelAI system administrator or the deployment team responsible for your installation.
