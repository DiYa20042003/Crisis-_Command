Privacy Policy

Last updated: April 2024

1. Information We Collect

SentinelAI collects video feed data from connected cameras for the sole purpose of real-time incident detection. No video is stored permanently. Incident snapshots are retained locally for audit purposes only.

2. How We Use Information

Collected data is used exclusively for crisis detection and staff notification within your hotel's network. We do not sell, share, or transmit personal data to third parties.

3. Email & SMS Notifications

When configured, email addresses and phone numbers are used solely to send incident alerts to authorized hotel staff. These credentials are stored as environment variables and never logged or shared.

4. Data Storage

Incident records are stored in a local SQLite database (or PostgreSQL if configured). You retain full control over this data and may delete it at any time.

5. Security

All communications between the dashboard and server use encrypted WebSocket connections. API keys and credentials are stored as server-side environment variables, never exposed to the client.

6. Contact

For privacy inquiries, contact your hotel's IT administrator or the system operator who deployed SentinelAI.
