The Process

Detect → Decide → Assign
→ Coordinate → Track → Report

Six automated stages replace manual radio calls, confusion and delayed responses.

01
Detect
AI models analyze live feed. Fire, violence, accidents and suspicious activity flagged in milliseconds.
02
Decide
Orchestration layer classifies severity — CRITICAL, HIGH or MEDIUM — and selects the right protocol.
03
Assign
Right personnel auto-dispatched in real time. Security, medical, manager — the exact roles needed.
04
Coordinate
Centralized dashboard keeps everyone aligned. Live status, locations and task assignments visible to all.
05
Track
Every action logged with timestamps. Real-time activity feed. Zone heatmaps show incident clusters.
06
Report
Automated incident reports generated and sent to management with response times and recommendations.
AI Detection Modules

Four Layers of
Intelligent Detection

🔥 Fire Detection
Multi-layer HSV color filtering with brightness validation, flicker analysis and edge irregularity checks. Eliminates false positives from skin tones and fabric.
CRITICAL SEVERITY
🥊 Violence Detection
Farneback optical flow + frame-difference burst analysis. Detects sudden chaotic motion spikes. No model download needed — pure mathematics.
HIGH SEVERITY
🚗 Accident Detection
YOLOv8n detects vehicles and persons. IoU overlap logic flags car-person and car-car collisions in real time. Falls back to motion blobs.
HIGH SEVERITY
🚨 Suspicious Activity
Crowd detection, intrusion zone monitoring, loitering tracking and after-hours activity flagging. YOLO-powered person counting eliminates furniture false positives.
MEDIUM SEVERITY
